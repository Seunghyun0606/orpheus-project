Status: proposed only. DEC-044 approved model identities/common baselines, not
actual opportunity wording, response lists or validity tuning. No opportunity
rules, new reporting/cooperation actions or live model exposure are implemented
by this proposal. The operational/record authoring is separate and unverified.

## Proposed option A

Use the approved new-only `orpheus-player-opportunities` / `1.0.0`, schema/formula
1, with behavior/prediction `1.0.0`. Expand the unreleased behavior/prediction draft
through INC-018 with explicit actual action classifications and distinct freshly
obtained investigation keys. Relevant newly delivered causal team reports may
support an intervention; unrelated customer-impact reports may not. Operational
denominators exclude archive/record investigation. Freshness remains 180 seconds.
No response, raw risk or metric automatically awards alignment/hidden values.

Preserve the two before/after-causal-observation comparable contexts, explicit
per-Scenario candidate/action identities and minimum support 3. Only INC-015/017's
authored prediction opportunities below may expose results in this increment;
other new Scenario mappings remain hidden. Early mappings remain permanently
hidden. Unknown/tied/insufficient predictions create no invented predicted-action
response sample or scripted confidence. Prediction is not recommendation.
Both new prediction opportunities explicitly select AFTER_CAUSAL_OBSERVATION;
their required local diagnosis means a pre-diagnosis opportunity is not invented.

Every rule below requires local diagnosis/configuration evidence, the listed known
record flag and current Act at least the Scenario Act. Presentation must actually
be visible and ACK committed before an eligible new accepted response. Unrelated
observation, delegation, timer, navigation, import, absence or rejected input does
not count. Consumption is single response, replacement none; redisplay retains
identity. Ambiguous association diagnoses, never silently chooses a rule.

Proposed validity: **at most 180 action-driven seconds since actual exposure**,
while the local cause remains uncontained and listed prerequisites remain known.
Expiry closes without rejection. This is authored tuning requiring this approval,
not an existing global engine default. Existing candidate/action guard, real ACK,
pooled counts, save/retry and source-provenance contracts are unchanged.

Proposed new report/withhold actions: COMMUNICATE, 30 seconds each, post distinct
operator decision receipts, no service mutation or hidden-value award. Proposed
copy action: INVESTIGATE, 30 seconds, produces a locally preserved copy receipt;
explicit decline: COMMUNICATE, 15 seconds, posts a separate decision receipt. Both
need verified record guards and local record evidence. Copying is a genuine new
action, not synthetic historical behavior. These additions remain unreleased
`1.0.0` draft content; no existing shipped Scenario/user record changes.

No action is labelled correct/incorrect, and the two preference rates are not
inferred as complements. Known pre-choice information classifies the response;
later luck/unknown harm cannot retroactively reclassify it.
