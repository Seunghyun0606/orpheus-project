# Codex brief — story art (2026-10-26)

Branch `codex/art`. Read `AGENTS.md` (section "Claude and Codex") first. Generate images with
your image generation tool. Do not change code; Claude wires the images into the UI afterwards.

## Shared style

Match the existing portraits in `godot/assets/portraits/` (look at several first). The game is a
night-shift incident-response techno-thriller at Vantage Systems, a payments and infrastructure
company. A colleague-run incident "bridge", a hidden company AI (ORPHEUS) and a presence in the
logs (NULL). The UI is a dark CRT console: near-black green (#0b0e0c), muted green-grey
(#111713), pale green text (#d6dfd2), amber accents (#ffb347), occasional cyan (#78c8ff) and red
(#ff7066). Painterly, semi-realistic, muted and desaturated; deep green-black shadows; cold
monitor glow plus one warm amber light; cinematic, slightly grainy, very faint scanlines. **No
text, letters, numbers, logos, readable screens or watermarks anywhere.** No recognisable real
people, brands or places.

## Images

Generate at the tool's landscape size (for example 1536×1024), then save as JPEG (quality about 85) scaled to the size given. Keep the originals out of the repository.

1. **Act cards**, `godot/assets/acts/act-1.jpg` … `act-9.jpg`, 1280×720, one per act, shown
   full-screen with the act title over the lower third (keep that area calm and dark):
   1 NORMAL: an ordinary night shift, the pager on a desk, nothing wrong yet.
   2 GHOST: a log line that should not exist; an empty desk lit by a terminal.
   3 NULL: a presence in the system; static gathering into a faint human outline.
   4 ORPHEUS: a vast server hall with one calm amber eye of light.
   5 EXPERIMENT: an observation room, one-way glass, the operator's desk seen from behind.
   6 HUMAN SYSTEM: hospitals, couriers and payroll as lit windows of a city, threads of light
   converging on one data centre.
   7 THE BOARD: an empty boardroom, a signed folder under a single lamp.
   8 OBSERVER: the player's desk seen from a high corner, like a camera watching.
   9 BLACKOUT: a city grid going dark district by district; one lit console remains.
2. **Ending illustrations**, `godot/assets/endings/<id>.jpg`, 1280×720, for ids `continuity`,
   `liberation`, `whistleblower`, `shutdown`, `custodian`, `null`, `the_board`, `the_fire`,
   `status_quo`, `rewrite`. Read each ending's text first: find the IDs in
   `src/story/data/inc-026.yaml` and the ending prose (search for the IDs under `src/story/` and
   `src/i18n/content/ko/`). Picture the world after that choice, not the act of choosing.
   Spoiler-safe for the credits screen.
3. **Title background**, `godot/assets/title.jpg`, 1920×1080: the night office from outside the
   glass, one lit workstation, very dark on the left half where the menu sits.
4. **Portrait variants** for after a reveal, 384×384 PNG like the existing portraits, same framing
   so they can replace the originals in place: `godot/assets/portraits/null-revealed.png` (the
   same lonely silhouette, now visibly made of the company's own server light, amber threads
   through the static) and `godot/assets/portraits/orpheus-active.png` (the same amber lens, now
   with countless thin lines of light reaching out from it into the racks).

Check every image yourself at full size; regenerate anything with text, a broken hand or face, or
a style that does not match.
